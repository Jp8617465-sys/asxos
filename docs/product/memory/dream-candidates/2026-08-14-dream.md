## Durable lessons (candidates)

### L-cand-14.1 — "Zero rows" is only evidence of failure if you never wrote down what success looks like
A pre-registered success criterion is what makes a **null result interpretable**. The 0043
runbook committed in advance to *"revision growth may be zero when the provider returns
byte-equivalent rows; what must be observed is that ingestion **succeeds**"* — so when the
scheduled `sync_prices` inside `daily-brief` run `31641460675` wrote 2,366 rows with the
capture trigger live and `price_revisions` stayed at **0**, the empty ledger read as
**confirmation**, not doubt. Without the pre-registration, the same observation supports both
"the trigger works and nothing needed capturing" and "the trigger is silently broken", and the
tiebreak would have been whichever the reader wanted.
**Evidence:** `decision-log.md:67` (`close-gov01-2026-08-12`); `roadmap-state.md:164` defect #3;
the mechanism is `_capture_price_revision`'s `to_jsonb(OLD) IS NOT DISTINCT FROM to_jsonb(NEW)`
early return.
**Generalises to:** any deadman, tripwire or drill whose healthy state is an empty result.
`proposed_target:` `approved-lessons.md`
